RECONCILIATION NOTES
1. The report is consolidated through September 2026, not a full-calendar-year 2026 activity total.
2. Latest September detail rows supersede older snapshots. Earlier rows, component rows and totals must not be added together.
3. Dashboard Heal 2,144,490 equals blood units 1,500,230 + health-checkup patients 483,439 + September 2025 eye OPD 160,821. This arithmetic match suggests a stale link and mixed units; the PDF does not expose spreadsheet formulas.
4. Dashboard Empower 2,038,847 matches the older tree count, not people. September 2026 tree count excluding Oneness Vann is 2,041,947.
5. Dashboard Projects 718,792 equals 600,330 plants + 5,962 water bodies + 112,500 impacted population; this arithmetic match suggests mixed units. It is not a person total.
6. The reported 2,642,277 trees equals 2,041,947 trees excluding Oneness Vann plus 600,330 Oneness Vann plants. The reported 4,218 plantation drives equals 3,518 + 700 Oneness Vann sites; these component labels differ.
7. The dashboard cleanliness drive count 7,809 equals railway 444 + hospitals 1,385 + WED 18 + waterbodies 5,962.
8. Blood-bank annual rows sum to 54,261 units and 444 camps, matching the cumulative total.
9. COVID totals retain their 2022 date; financial help retains its 15-May-2025 date. They are included because they remain the latest records in the consolidated report, not relabelled as 2026 activity.
10. Village overview population 112,500 and school children 10,000 differ in scope from village detail population 35,127, beneficiaries 29,627 and students 7,750. Preserve both without combining.
11. There is no deduplicated count of lives impacted across Heal, Enrich and Empower in this PDF. Potential lives, patients, learners, volunteers, couples, supplies, trees, hours and money describe different measures.
12. Chiropractic patient total 83,146 exists elsewhere in the website but does not appear in this five-page PDF and is excluded from this extraction.
